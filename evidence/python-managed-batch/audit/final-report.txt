   cancellation dispatch, after unrecovered failure hooks. Explicit before-settle
   AbortResult also cancels. Both sync/async settle drivers handle dispatch_cancel.
   Original signer/application errors survive, and cancel-phase errors do not
   recursively cancel. The dispatch flag is set before the facilitator call;
   thrown/unknown remote outcomes are not automatically canceled.
2. Pending/failed outcomes retain trusted reservation identity and refund snapshot.
   The existing exactly-one settlement_pending retry is unchanged. A later
   explicit reconciliation attempt can enrich the same reservation. Failed cancel
   retains identity; confirmed success/cancel and weakref payload collection clear
   request state. Receipt-bearing or settlement_pending local SettleError is
   excluded from automatic cancel. Manual failure recovery still short-circuits.
3. A trusted pendingId is captured before validating a managed verify snapshot.
   Missing or noncanonical required uint fields fail before the handler. Known
   admission is canceled; missing identity is never invented or borrowed. Exact
   Python integers/decimal strings preserve a full-width refund nonce.
4. Authoritative receipt fields are parsed before the optional replica update.
   Missing/malformed authority yields AfterSettleAbortError with the same receipt's
   transaction/amount/extra retained and no cancellation of its committed outcome.
   Optional storage/callback failures instead log once and preserve success. The
   real buyer response hook advances its local watermark from the known receipt.
   Receipt validation does not depend on a cached local request context.
5. The coordinator restored direct voucher/deposit settle without local verify:
   absent pendingId reaches Go's existing full-reverify path. Replay again reaches
   the facilitator and is rejected without another charge. Refund enrichment
   continues to require its verified snapshot. No extra protocol restriction was
   introduced by the cleanup correction.
